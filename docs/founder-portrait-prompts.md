## Initial prompt

Edit the reference photo into a professional, photorealistic chest-up profile portrait of the SAME PERSON. Identity preservation is the highest priority: retain exact facial proportions, age, skin tone, hair shape and texture, facial hair, glasses and distinctive features. Do not beautify or change the jaw or nose. Keep the existing head angle and natural expression rather than inventing a new face. Centered head and shoulders, complete hair with generous margin, shoulders to lower chest, square canvas. Soft even studio lighting, realistic skin texture. Understated smart casual plain dark navy crew-neck knit, no suit, tie, logos or text. Remove all scenery and furniture. GENUINELY TRANSPARENT background with alpha, clean natural hair edges, no white matte, no checkerboard printed into image, no shadow behind subject. Consistent restrained professional photography, not illustration. One person only.

## Alpha extraction correction

Background removal only. Deliver a PNG cutout with a real alpha channel: all white background pixels outside the person must be fully transparent (alpha 0). Preserve the foreground person exactly, including every facial feature, hair strands, glasses, clothing, colors, pose and framing. No regeneration of the face. No background of any color. Do not draw a checkerboard or simulate transparency visually. Actual transparent image output, with soft alpha at hair edges, no white fringe or drop shadow.
